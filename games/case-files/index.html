How a Case Works

01 — Investigate

Search the scene and collect objective evidence. Important clues are preserved exactly as found.

02 — Deduce

Compare statements with timestamps, physical evidence and alibis. Red herrings have explanations.

03 — Accuse

Name the culprit, method and timeline. A correct solution should follow from evidence—not guessing.
