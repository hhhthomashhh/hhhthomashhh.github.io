CASE 002

The Vanished Painting

At a private gallery preview, the insured masterpiece Harbour at Dusk disappears during an 86-second blackout. The gallery was sealed, the frame alarm never sounded, and four people had reasons to want the painting gone.

CASE 002
THE VANISHED
PAINTING
The lights went out. The painting was gone.
The theft may have happened much earlier.

What happened

At 9:01 PM the gallery loses power for 86 seconds. When emergency lighting stabilizes, Harbour at Dusk is missing from its frame. Guests are held inside while security seals the building.

The puzzle

No alarm sounded. No one is recorded leaving with a large canvas. The gallery owner insists the painting was on the wall immediately before the blackout.

Your job

Determine who stole the real painting, when it actually left secure custody, how the disappearance was staged, and why.

Detective standard

Do not assume the most dramatic event is the crime. Compare photographs, logs, physical traces and each suspect’s reason for lying.
