CASE 001

The Missing Diamond

At a private dinner in Ashcroft House, the 18-carat Blue Meridian diamond is discovered missing from its locked glass display. The hallway camera shows no one entering the study after 8:45 PM. Four guests had motive. One of them built the perfect alibi around the wrong assumption.

What happened

At 9:06 PM, gem appraiser Evelyn Shaw looks through the display glass and immediately says the stone is not the Blue Meridian. A later inspection confirms the stone in the case is a near-perfect cubic zirconia replica.

Your job: identify who stole the real diamond, when the switch happened, and how.

The apparent impossibility

The study was placed under continuous hallway-camera watch at 8:45 PM. From then until the discovery, nobody entered or left the room. The display lock shows no forced entry.

Do not assume the crime happened when it was discovered.

Rules of the case

Every fact needed to solve the case can be found in the game. Red herrings may explain suspicious behavior, but the final answer does not depend on guessing personality or motive.

Detective standard

A strong solution should establish: last confirmed genuine → first confirmed fake → who had opportunity in between → evidence of preparation.
