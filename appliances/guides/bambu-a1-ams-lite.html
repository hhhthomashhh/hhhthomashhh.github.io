Bambu Lab A1 + AMS lite

Open-frame 3D printer with four-spool AMS lite multi-colour system. This local guide preserves the important ownership and maintenance notes while the official Bambu wiki remains the primary detailed service source.

Key specifications

  • Build volume: 256 × 256 × 256 mm
  • Standard nozzle: 0.4 mm
  • Maximum hotend temperature: 300°C
  • Maximum bed temperature: 100°C
  • AMS lite supports up to four filament spools for automatic material/colour changes.

Routine maintenance

  • Keep the build plate clean. Wash with warm water and dish soap when adhesion declines; avoid touching the print surface with oily fingers.
  • Inspect PTFE tubes and filament paths for grooves, kinks or wear.
  • Remove filament dust and fragments from the extruder/feeder area when needed.
  • Follow Bambu's lubrication reminders for moving components and inspect belts for correct tension and damage.
  • Keep AMS lite spool holders and feeder entrances free of filament debris.

Useful troubleshooting

  • Poor first layer: clean plate, confirm correct plate selected in slicer, then rerun calibration/bed leveling.
  • Filament feed errors: inspect spool winding, PTFE path, AMS feeder and extruder for jams.
  • Repeated nozzle clogs: verify filament temperature/profile and inspect the nozzle/hotend.
  • Print shifts or unusual motion noise: stop the print and inspect belts, rails and foreign debris before continuing.
