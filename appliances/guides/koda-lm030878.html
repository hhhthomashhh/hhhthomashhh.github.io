KODA LM030878 / LM030878F

Motion Activated LED Security Floodlight · Costco item 1938035 · three fixtures installed outside the garage. This guide is based on the retained printed booklet plus KODA's current support information.

Key specifications

  • Maximum output: 4,800 lumens
  • Hardwired 120 V outdoor fixture
  • Three adjustable LED light heads
  • Motion detection up to approximately 80 ft / 24.4 m
  • Detection coverage up to approximately 240°
  • Adjustable brightness, timer, ambient-light threshold and motion sensitivity

Recommended garage setup

  • Brightness: full
  • Timer: 1 or 3 minutes
  • LUX: sunset / low-light
  • SENS: medium
  • If traffic, trees or pedestrians cause false triggers, aim the sensor lower and reduce sensitivity.

Controls

  • Brightness: full or low output.
  • TIME: 1, 3 or 7 minutes after motion.
  • LUX: controls whether the light may operate in daylight, low light, or only darkness.
  • SENS: low, medium or high motion-detection range.

Manual override

  • To force the light ON: toggle the wall switch OFF → ON → OFF → ON within about 3 seconds.
  • The fixture remains on for roughly 6 hours, then returns to preset mode.
  • To cancel manual mode: turn the wall switch OFF for more than 10 seconds, then turn it ON.
  • After power-up, allow roughly 20 seconds for warm-up/initialization.

Troubleshooting

  • Will not turn on: check wall switch, breaker/fuse, wiring, sensor aim and LUX setting.
  • Comes on in daytime: move LUX toward the darker/moon setting or check whether the sensor is in a shaded location.
  • Stays on continuously: cancel manual override; redirect light heads away from the sensor and nearby heat sources; check wiring if the problem persists.
  • False triggers: reduce SENS, aim sensor lower, and keep moving branches/traffic outside the detection zone.
  • One fixture behaves differently: compare all four control switches across the three fixtures and standardize their settings.

Outdoor sealing & safety

  • Use exterior silicone around the mounting plate/mounting surface, but leave an uncaulked gap at the bottom for drainage.
  • Do not use a dimmer or timer as the fixture's control switch.
  • Turn the circuit breaker OFF before opening the electrical box or working on wiring.

Cleaning

  • Use clear water and a soft damp cloth only.
  • Do not use paints, solvents, harsh chemicals, a garden hose directed at the fixture or a pressure washer.
  • Remove spider webs and debris from the sensor face and verify that the bottom drainage gap remains open.
