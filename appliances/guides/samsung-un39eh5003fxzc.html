Samsung UN39EH5003FXZC

39-inch Full HD LED television · manufactured August 2013. This locally stored guide preserves the useful model and care information even if Samsung later retires the support page.

Identification

  • Model: UN39EH5003FXZC
  • 39-inch class LED-backlit LCD television
  • 1920 × 1080 Full HD panel
  • 60 Hz class television
  • Two HDMI inputs plus USB and legacy AV connections
  • Non-smart TV; a modern HDMI streaming box can add current apps.

Care

  • Unplug before cleaning.
  • Use a clean, soft microfiber cloth on the panel and cabinet.
  • Do not spray cleaner or water directly onto the television.
  • Keep ventilation slots open and periodically remove accumulated dust from the rear vents.

Basic troubleshooting

  • No picture: confirm the correct Source/Input and check the HDMI cable at both ends.
  • No power: check wall outlet, power cord and standby indicator before assuming an internal failure.
  • Streaming device picture problems: power-cycle both TV and HDMI device, then reconnect HDMI.
  • Remote problems: replace batteries and confirm nothing blocks the TV's remote sensor.
