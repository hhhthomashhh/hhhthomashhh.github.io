Routine care

  • Have the boiler inspected and serviced periodically by a qualified heating technician, especially before the heating season.
  • Keep the combustion-air intake and exhaust vent terminals clear of debris, snow, nests and other blockage.
  • Check the system pressure gauge periodically and investigate persistent pressure loss or unusually high pressure rather than repeatedly topping up.
  • Inspect around the boiler, condensate drain and nearby piping for leaks, corrosion or staining.
  • Clean or service strainers, filters and the condensate system according to the manufacturer's maintenance schedule and the home's water quality.
  • For domestic-hot-water performance, scale buildup may require flushing/descaling depending on hardness and usage.

Useful operating notes

  • The front display may show boiler water temperature or operating information; a displayed temperature should not automatically be interpreted as the domestic hot-water setpoint.
  • If an error code appears, record the exact code before resetting the boiler; repeated faults should be diagnosed rather than repeatedly reset.
  • Do not close isolation valves or change gas, venting, combustion or safety settings unless you understand the system and the manufacturer's procedure.
Model confirmation: the i120C identification is based on the unit's visible 95.5% AFUE EnergyGuide label and the matching I-Series manual. For parts ordering or service, confirm the model and serial number on the boiler's factory data plate.
