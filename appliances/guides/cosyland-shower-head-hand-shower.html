COSYLAND Shower Head with Hand Shower

Rainfall shower head + handheld shower combination with extension arm, hose, adhesive hand-shower holder and 3-way diverter. This guide is preserved locally from the owner's 4-page instruction booklet, with the same manual also located online.

Included parts

  • Hand shower
  • Square rainfall shower head
  • Metal hose
  • Washers
  • Hand-shower wall mount / holder
  • Pipe sealant (PTFE) tape
  • 3-way diverter
  • Adjustable extension shower arm

Normal operation

  • The 3-way diverter lets you select the fixed/rain shower, the hand shower, or both together.
  • The extension arm can be adjusted for height and spray angle. Loosen the swing knobs by hand, position the arm/head, then hand-tighten to lock it.
  • A small amount of dripping after the shower is shut off can occur as water remaining in the large shower head drains.

Installation summary

  1. Remove the old shower head.
  2. Wrap the male pipe threads with PTFE tape, overlapping about half the tape width. Keep tape on the threads and do not extend it into the water passage.
  3. Confirm a washer is present at the inlet connection, then attach the 3-way diverter to the shower-arm pipe and hand-tighten.
  4. Install the extension arm onto the existing shower arm. Ensure the black rubber washer is inside the extension-arm nut.
  5. Install the rainfall shower head on the other end of the extension arm. Use PTFE tape on the threaded connection and make sure the washer is present.
  6. Set the desired height and angle, then hand-tighten the two swing knobs.
  7. Install the hose to the diverter with a washer at the connection.
  8. Connect the other end of the hose to the hand shower, again with the washer in place.
  9. Before sticking the hand-shower holder to the wall, check its orientation. The wall must be clean, smooth and completely dry. Press the adhesive mount firmly in place and leave it unloaded for 24 hours before hanging the hand shower.
  10. Turn on the water and check every connection for leaks or looseness.

Important installation notes

  • Do not force or over-tighten the square rainfall shower head. The booklet warns that excessive force can damage the shower-head thread and cause leakage.
  • The adhesive hand-shower mount is not intended for uneven wall surfaces.
  • If a threaded joint remains loose, replace/reapply the PTFE tape.
  • If a connection leaks after the installation steps have been checked, confirm the washer is present; adding/replacing a washer may solve the leak.

Cleaning & maintenance

  • Use mild soap and a soft cloth.
  • Do not use ammonia, scouring/detergent powder, toilet cleaners, abrasive cleaners or other aggressive chemicals that may damage the finish.
  • If the rainfall head becomes clogged, first rub the silicone nozzles to break up mineral deposits.
  • If still clogged, shut off the water, remove the shower head, remove and rinse the washer, and flush/rinse the inside of the head with non-acidic water before reinstalling the washer and head.
  • For the handheld shower, similarly rub the silicone nozzles first; if needed, remove the hand shower, rinse its washer, and flush the hand shower and hose with non-acidic water.

Troubleshooting

Leak at a threaded joint

  • Check that the connection is straight and hand-tight.
  • Confirm the rubber washer is installed.
  • Reapply PTFE tape to male threads if needed.
  • Avoid excessive tightening.

Poor / uneven spray

  • Rub the silicone spray holes.
  • Remove mineral debris from the washer/screens.
  • Flush the shower head and hose.
  • Check that PTFE tape is not obstructing the water passage.

Hand-shower holder falls off

  • Remove old adhesive residue and dry the wall completely.
  • Use only on a smooth, even surface.
  • After mounting, wait 24 hours before loading it.

Diverter not working normally

  • Confirm it is threaded on squarely and not over-tightened.
  • Check for debris or obstruction.
  • If the internal diverter fails, replacement is generally more practical than disassembly.
