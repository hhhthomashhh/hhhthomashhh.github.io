Whirlpool WX46500 · Type 111

Older Whirlpool top-load washer using the classic direct-drive motor/transmission system. This local reference records the machine identification, the September 2026 motor-coupler repair, safe testing steps and the most useful service documentation.

Machine identification

  • Brand: Whirlpool
  • Model: WX46500
  • Type: 111
  • Configuration: top-load washer
  • Drive system: Whirlpool direct-drive motor-to-transmission design
  • Electrical: standard household 120 V supply

September 2026 repair

  • Symptom: washer would fill and drain, but the agitator/basket would not operate normally.
  • The old motor coupler was removed and replaced.
  • The replacement coupler uses two plastic hubs and a flexible rubber centre piece between the motor and transmission.
  • Both coupler hubs must be fully seated on their shafts and aligned before the motor is clipped back into place.
  • The four motor-mount rubber isolators must remain correctly seated during reassembly.

Safe post-repair test

  1. With the washer unplugged, confirm the motor clips, pump clips, wiring connector, cabinet and lid-switch connector are secure.
  2. Reconnect the water and drain hoses, turn the water on and check for leaks.
  3. Run an empty small-load/low-water cycle.
  4. Confirm normal fill and agitation for a minute or two.
  5. Allow the washer to drain and enter spin. Stop immediately for loud grinding, severe knocking, burning smell or violent vibration.
  6. If the empty cycle is normal, test next with two or three towels before returning to a full load.

Safety

  • Unplug the washer before opening the cabinet or touching the motor, pump, coupler, transmission or wiring.
  • Do not run the machine with hands or tools near moving components.
  • The L-55 service manual is a platform-level technician job aid; wiring and details can vary by model. Use the washer's own wiring diagram/tech sheet when model-specific information conflicts.
