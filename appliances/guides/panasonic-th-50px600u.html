Panasonic VIERA TH-50PX600U

50-inch plasma television · released 2006 · native 1366 × 768. This page is stored locally on the website so the essential operating and care information remains available even if the manufacturer support page changes.

Identification

  • Model: TH-50PX600U / TH-50PX600 family
  • Display: 50-inch plasma
  • Native panel: 1366 × 768
  • HD input compatibility includes 720p and 1080i, scaled to the panel
  • Two HDMI inputs

Everyday use

  • For modern streaming devices, connect by HDMI and set the source to 720p or 1080i if you want to minimize unnecessary scaling.
  • Plasma displays are best used with varied content; avoid leaving bright static logos, menus, game HUDs or paused images on screen for very long periods.
  • If image retention is seen, switch to varied full-screen video for a while rather than displaying another static image.

Care

  • Turn the TV off and allow it to cool before cleaning.
  • Use a soft dry or lightly damp microfiber cloth. Do not spray liquid directly onto the panel.
  • Keep the rear ventilation openings clear of dust and allow generous airflow around the cabinet.
  • Do not block vents or operate in a tightly enclosed cabinet because plasma TVs generate substantial heat.

Moving / safety

  • This is a very heavy television. Use at least two capable adults to move it.
  • Keep it upright during transport and avoid twisting or flexing the glass panel.
  • Disconnect power and cables before moving.
