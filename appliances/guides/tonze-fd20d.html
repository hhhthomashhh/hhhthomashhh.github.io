TONZE / 天际 FD20D

Micro-computer rice cooker with ceramic inner pot. The owner also retains the printed operation manual.

Key specifications

  • Model: FD20D
  • Capacity: 2.0 L / approximately 4 cups
  • Power: 350 W
  • Electrical: 120 V, 60 Hz
  • Approximate dimensions: 304 × 254 × 217 mm
  • Ceramic inner cooking pot

Main cooking functions

  • Regular rice
  • Porridge / congee
  • Soup
  • Baby porridge
  • Clay-pot style rice
  • Preset/timer cooking

Before cooking

  • Make sure the underside of the ceramic pot and the heating plate are clean and dry.
  • Seat the pot flat in the cooker before closing the lid.
  • Do not operate the cooker with foreign material trapped between the pot and heating plate.

Ceramic pot care

  • Allow the hot ceramic pot to cool before adding cold water; abrupt temperature changes can crack ceramic.
  • Use wood, silicone or plastic utensils rather than metal utensils.
  • Avoid abrasive scouring pads and harsh cleansers.
  • Inspect for cracks or chips before use. Replace a damaged ceramic pot.

Basic troubleshooting

  • No power/display: verify outlet and power connection.
  • Rice cooks unevenly: confirm the pot is seated flat, heating plate is clean, and water/rice quantities are correct.
  • Food sticks or scorches: check recipe proportions and ensure the cooker did not run with too little liquid.
  • Steam or liquid escapes excessively: check lid seating, cleanliness and fill level.

Safety

  • Do not immerse the cooker body, cord or plug in water.
  • Unplug before cleaning and allow hot components to cool.
  • Keep steam vents unobstructed and avoid placing hands/face over escaping steam.
