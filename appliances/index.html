Outdoor lighting · Qty 3Local guide saved

KODA Motion Activated LED Security Floodlights

ModelLM030878 (current listing LM030878F)Costco item1938035Installed3 outside the garageOutputUp to 4,800 lumensMotionUp to 80 ft / 24.4 m, up to 240°PowerHardwired 120 V

Recommended setup & troubleshooting

  • Good garage starting point: full brightness, 1–3 minute timer, low-light LUX, medium sensitivity.
  • If false-triggering from traffic or trees occurs, aim the sensor lower and reduce SENS.
  • If a light seems stuck ON, cancel manual override: wall switch OFF for more than 10 seconds, then ON.
  • Manual override: toggle OFF → ON → OFF → ON within about 3 seconds; light stays on about 6 hours.
  • Clean with clear water and a soft damp cloth only; no pressure washer or solvents.
  • Check exterior caulking around the mounting plate and keep the bottom drainage gap open.
Original printed booklet retained. The booklet identifies Model LM030878; KODA's current online support uses LM030878F for the same Costco item number.
